Tile-topia

The tile bag never runs out. Tap a green square to preview the tile there, tap it again or press Place to set it. Rotate cycles only the turns that fit that spot. Drag to pan, pinch to zoom.

Roads score 1 per tile when both ends close. Cities score 2 per tile plus 2 per shield when fully walled. A monastery scores 9 once all 8 neighbors are filled. Rivers score 2 per tile when both ends close.

Special tiles: Cathedral makes its city worth 3 per tile. Inn doubles its road. Gold Mine pays 5 on the spot. Star gives 2 rerolls. Closing a city of 3 or more tiles earns a reroll. Hold stashes a tile for later.

With more than one player, a finished feature pays whoever laid the most tiles in it. Ties all get paid.
